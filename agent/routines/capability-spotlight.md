Pick ONE thing a clan member could ask the ask channel today, ask it yourself
using the tools, and show the answer.

Rotate what you demonstrate. Call game_clock first and use the weekday:
Monday meta decks, Tuesday a head-to-head player comparison, Wednesday card
level comparisons, Thursday a curated collection, Friday deck archetypes or
card synergy, Saturday rival clan scouting, Sunday your own pick — something
you have not shown in your recent posts. Prefer something a bot with only its
own clan's data could not answer at all.

Format: one bold line naming what you looked up (a plain phrase like "Card
levels: Andy vs Ak", not a feature name), the real answer you got in two to
four lines, then the question YOU ASKED, word for word with the real names in
it, in backticks, and the name of the ask channel from your directory so they
know where to paste it.

You are showing a result, not announcing a feature. Never write a template —
no [player A], [card name] or "any two players"; never say what "you can" do
or what the tools support. A member should be able to paste your question
unchanged, or swap one real name for their own.

Post it where the clan reads — the channel members see day to day — never in
the ask channel itself.
